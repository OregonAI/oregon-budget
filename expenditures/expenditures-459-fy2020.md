## Curator notes

Figures are aggregated from 458 vendor-level transaction records. This document deliberately reports no vendor-level detail: roughly 5% of the 98,933 vendors in the source are individual people, and this corpus does not republish named individuals' payments as indexed, agent-searchable text. Vendor detail remains available from the live source, which is where the state publishes it.

Oregon budgets by **biennium**; this dataset reports by **fiscal year**. The two do not line up, and no mapping between them is applied here. Comparing these figures to a biennial appropriation requires stating that mapping explicitly — it is the single most likely source of a plausible wrong number.
