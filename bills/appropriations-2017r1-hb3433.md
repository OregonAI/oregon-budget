# Appropriations in 2017 Regular Session House Bill 3433

## At a glance

HB 3433 (2017R1): Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Oregon Department of Administrative Services for distribution to Stillwagon Distillery for developing process for recycling and reusing distillery spent wash.

Parsed context: appropriated to **Oregon Department of Administrative Services**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2017** (fiscal years 2018–2019).
