# Appropriations in 2017 Regular Session House Bill 5002

## At a glance

HB 5002 (2017R1): Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Oregon Department of Administrative Services for certain biennial expenses.

Parsed context: appropriated to **Oregon Department of Administrative Services**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2017** (fiscal years 2018–2019).

Extraction status: **items-without-stated-total**. No itemization to reconcile against.

This bill appropriates in **4 separate sections**. Each is reconciled on its own: item numbering restarts per section, so amounts must never be pooled across them.

The full text of this bill lives in the `oregon-legislature` corpus as `measure-2017r1-hb5002` and is referenced, not copied.

## Curator notes

Summing every dollar figure in an appropriation bill **double-counts**: a bill states an appropriation and then itemizes the same money. The stated appropriation and the line items are separate tables above for exactly that reason, and must never be added together.

The 'purpose' column is a parser's reading of the surrounding prose, not the bill's own words. The verbatim source line beside it is the bill's own words, and is the column to trust.
