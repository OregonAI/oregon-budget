# Appropriations in 2017 Regular Session House Bill 3151

## At a glance

HB 3151 (2017R1): Appropriates moneys to State Forestry Department for 2017-2019 biennium to carry out integrated pest management program to combat Sudden Oak Death.

Parsed context: appropriated to **State Forestry Department**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2017** (fiscal years 2018–2019).
