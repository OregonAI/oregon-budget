# Appropriations in 2019 Regular Session House Bill 3326

## At a glance

HB 3326 (2019R1): Appropriates moneys to Department of Environmental Quality to fund laboratory services for public water systems that are susceptible to hazardous algal blooms and to Oregon Health Authority to hire recreational hazardous algal bloom coordinator.

Parsed context: appropriated to **Department of Environmental Quality**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2019** (fiscal years 2020–2021).

Extraction status: **single-appropriation**. No itemization to reconcile against.
