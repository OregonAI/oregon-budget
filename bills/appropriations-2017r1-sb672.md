# Appropriations in 2017 Regular Session Senate Bill 672

## At a glance

SB 672 (2017R1): Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Department of Education for purpose of awarding grants under Career and Technical Education Revitalization Grant Program.

Parsed context: appropriated to **Department of Education**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2017** (fiscal years 2018–2019).

Extraction status: **single-appropriation**. No itemization to reconcile against.
