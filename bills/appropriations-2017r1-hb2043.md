# Appropriations in 2017 Regular Session House Bill 2043

## At a glance

HB 2043 (2017R1): Appropriates moneys for 2017-2019 biennium to State Department of Agriculture for purpose of carrying out weed control programs.

Parsed context: appropriated to **State Department of Agriculture**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2017** (fiscal years 2018–2019).

## Curator notes

Summing every dollar figure in an appropriation bill **double-counts**: a bill states an appropriation and then itemizes the same money. The stated appropriation and the line items are separate tables above for exactly that reason, and must never be added together.
