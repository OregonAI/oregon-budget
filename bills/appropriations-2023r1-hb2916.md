# Appropriations in 2023 Regular Session House Bill 2916

## At a glance

HB 2916 (2023R1): Appropriates moneys from General Fund to State Marine Board and Department of State Lands for salvage, towing, storage and disposal of abandoned vessels and derelict vessels.

Parsed context: appropriated to **State Marine Board**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2023** (fiscal years 2024–2025).

Extraction status: **single-appropriation**. No itemization to reconcile against.

This bill appropriates in **2 separate sections**. Each is reconciled on its own: item numbering restarts per section, so amounts must never be pooled across them.
