# Appropriations in 2023 Regular Session House Bill 2485

## At a glance

HB 2485 (2023R1): Requires Portland State University, subject to sufficient moneys being appropriated, to enhance state's capacity to educate public mental health and addiction treatment providers by July 1, 2028, by materially increasing number of graduates from relevant degree programs.
