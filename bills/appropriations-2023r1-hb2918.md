# Appropriations in 2023 Regular Session House Bill 2918

## At a glance

HB 2918 (2023R1): Appropriates moneys to Oregon Health Authority to use to continue pilot program providing grants to entities to operate mobile health units.

Parsed context: appropriated to **Oregon Health Authority**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2023** (fiscal years 2024–2025).
