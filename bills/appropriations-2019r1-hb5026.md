# Appropriations in 2019 Regular Session House Bill 5026

## At a glance

HB 5026 (2019R1): Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Department of Human Services for certain biennial expenses.

Parsed context: appropriated to **Department of Human Services**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2019** (fiscal years 2020–2021).

## Curator notes

Summing every dollar figure in an appropriation bill **double-counts**: a bill states an appropriation and then itemizes the same money. The stated appropriation and the line items are separate tables above for exactly that reason, and must never be added together.
