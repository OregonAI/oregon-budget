# Appropriations in 2019 Regular Session Senate Bill 265

## At a glance

SB 265 (2019R1): Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Department of Education for purpose of awarding grants under Career and Technical Education Revitalization Grant Program.

Parsed context: appropriated to **Department of Education**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2019** (fiscal years 2020–2021).

Extraction status: **single-appropriation**. No itemization to reconcile against.
