# Appropriations in 2017 Regular Session Senate Bill 610

## At a glance

SB 610 (2017R1): Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Higher Education Coordinating Commission for Office of Community Colleges and Workforce Development for Oregon Employer Workforce Training Program, local workforce investment boards and Oregon Youth Conservation Corps.

Parsed context: appropriated to **Higher Education Coordinating Commission**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2017** (fiscal years 2018–2019).
