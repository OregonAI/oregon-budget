# Appropriations in 2019 Regular Session House Bill 2365

## At a glance

HB 2365 (2019R1): Appropriates moneys to State Forestry Department for 2019-2021 biennium to carry out integrated pest management program to combat Sudden Oak Death.

Parsed context: appropriated to **State Forestry Department**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2019** (fiscal years 2020–2021).
