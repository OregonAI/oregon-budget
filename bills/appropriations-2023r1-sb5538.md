# Appropriations in 2023 Regular Session Senate Bill 5538

## At a glance

SB 5538 (2023R1): Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Department of Veterans' Affairs for certain biennial expenses.

Parsed context: appropriated to **Department of Veterans’ Affairs**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2023** (fiscal years 2024–2025).
