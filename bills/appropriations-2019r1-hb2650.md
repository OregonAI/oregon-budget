# Appropriations in 2019 Regular Session House Bill 2650

## At a glance

HB 2650 (2019R1): Appropriates moneys to Oregon Military Department to expand and increase access to statewide 2-1-1 system.

Parsed context: appropriated to **Oregon Military Department**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2019** (fiscal years 2020–2021).
