# Appropriations in 2017 Regular Session House Bill 2984

## At a glance

HB 2984 (2017R1): Appropriates moneys to Oregon Watershed Enhancement Board for 2017-2019 biennium for issuing grant to Curry County for implementing replacement of control devices at discharge from Floras Lake into Floras Creek.

Parsed context: appropriated to **Oregon Watershed Enhancement Board**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2017** (fiscal years 2018–2019).

Extraction status: **single-appropriation**. No itemization to reconcile against.
