# Appropriations in 2017 Regular Session Senate Bill 5533

## At a glance

SB 5533 (2017R1): Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Oregon Department of Administrative Services for debt service on outstanding general obligation bonds sold pursuant to Article XI-G of Oregon Constitution for Oregon Health and Science University.

Parsed context: appropriated to **Oregon Department of Administrative Services**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2017** (fiscal years 2018–2019).

Extraction status: **single-appropriation**. No itemization to reconcile against.
