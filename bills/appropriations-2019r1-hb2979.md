# Appropriations in 2019 Regular Session House Bill 2979

## At a glance

HB 2979 (2019R1): Appropriates moneys to Oregon Department of Administrative Services for distribution to Association of Oregon Counties to obtain and manage contracted services for provision of technical assistance and information for private landowners to facilitate compliance with certain requirements related to replacement or repair of tide gates, with priority given to tide gates with opening size of four feet or smaller.

Parsed context: appropriated to **Oregon Department of Administrative Services**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2019** (fiscal years 2020–2021).
