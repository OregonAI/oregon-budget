# Appropriations in 2019 Regular Session Senate Bill 344

## At a glance

SB 344 (2019R1): Appropriates moneys from General Fund to State Workforce and Talent Development Board for distribution to local workforce development boards.

Parsed context: appropriated to **State Workforce and Talent Development Board**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2019** (fiscal years 2020–2021).
