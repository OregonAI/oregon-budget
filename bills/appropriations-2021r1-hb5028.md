# Appropriations in 2021 Regular Session House Bill 5028

## At a glance

HB 5028 (2021R1): Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Department of State Police for certain biennial expenses.

Parsed context: appropriated to **Department of State Police**, out of the **General Fund**, for the biennium **beginning 2021** (fiscal years 2022–2023).
