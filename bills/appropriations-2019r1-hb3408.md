# Appropriations in 2019 Regular Session House Bill 3408

## At a glance

HB 3408 (2019R1): Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Department of Environmental Quality for deposit in Residential Solid Fuel Heating Air Quality Improvement Fund to fund community efforts to promote economic development and improve public health by reducing emissions from solid fuel burning devices that burn wood.
